Terms of use

Last updated: 17 September 2026

By creating an account or using BloodConnect, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

1. What BloodConnect is

BloodConnect helps hospitals contact nearby voluntary blood donors. It is not a blood bank, a medical provider or an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112.

2. Donors

  • You must be at least 18 years old and give accurate information about yourself.
  • Answer the eligibility questions honestly. The blood bank's medical officer makes the final decision on whether you can donate.
  • If you pledge and then can't go, please cancel the pledge so the hospital can find someone else.
  • Donation is voluntary. You must not ask for or accept payment for blood.

3. Hospitals

  • Only register an organisation you're authorised to represent.
  • Only raise requests for genuine clinical need, and close them once they're fulfilled.
  • Use donor contact details and arrival times only to coordinate the donation they pledged for.

4. Accounts

Keep your password private. We may suspend accounts that give false information, misuse donor details or send misleading requests.

5. No guarantees

We work hard to keep BloodConnect available and accurate, but we can't guarantee that a donor will respond or that blood will be available. We aren't responsible for medical decisions or outcomes.

6. Privacy

Our privacy policy explains what data we collect and how it's used.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. We'll change the date at the top of this page when we do.
