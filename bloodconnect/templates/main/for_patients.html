For patients and families

Getting blood when someone you love needs it

Blood requests on BloodConnect are raised by hospitals, so the treating team can confirm what's needed and handle the transfusion safely.

{{ ui.icon('alert') }}

In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. BloodConnect doesn't replace emergency services.

How to get help

  1. Talk to the treating doctor or the hospital's blood bank. They'll confirm the blood group and the number of units.
  2. Ask whether the hospital uses BloodConnect. If it doesn't, the hospital can register for free.
  3. The hospital posts the request and alerts compatible donors nearby.
  4. Donors pledge and go to the hospital, where the blood bank screens and tests every donation.

Good to know

  • All donated blood is screened and tested before it's used.
  • We can't guarantee that a donor will be available, but we help hospitals reach as many compatible donors as possible, quickly.
  • Patient details stay with the hospital. Donors only see the hospital, blood group, urgency and units needed.
  • You should never be asked to pay a donor. If anyone asks for money, tell the hospital.
