How it works

From an urgent request to a donor at the door

See the full process {{ ui.icon('arrow-right') }}
  1. Request

    A hospital posts a request

    The blood bank enters the blood group, the units needed and how urgent it is.

  2. Match

    Compatible donors nearby are alerted

    We match by blood group, distance, availability and the time since each donor last gave blood.

  3. Pledge

    You pledge and get directions

    A short eligibility check, one tap to pledge, then live directions. The hospital sees your ETA, never your location.

  4. Donate

    The blood bank confirms

    Your donation is recorded, the request updates for the hospital, and you earn your next badge.
