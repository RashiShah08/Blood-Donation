How it works

From an urgent request to a donor at the door

A request goes from the hospital to compatible donors nearby, and the donor goes from pledging to the blood bank.

For hospitals

  1. Register your hospital with its address and location on the map.
  2. Create a request with the patient's blood group, units needed and urgency. Patient details are only visible to your hospital.
  3. Search nearby donors. You'll see how many available, compatible donors are within 2, 5, 10 or 25 km, shown at approximate positions.
  4. Alert donors. Each donor is emailed at most once per request.
  5. Follow arrivals live. The operations console shows who pledged and each donor's live arrival time. Record who donated, and close the request when it's fulfilled.

For donors

  1. Register with your blood group, weight, date of birth and home area.
  2. See requests you can help with on your dashboard. New ones appear live, or you'll get an email.
  3. Answer a short eligibility check based on India's national guidelines. Your answers aren't stored.
  4. Pledge one unit and follow live directions. The hospital sees your ETA, not your location.
  5. Donate. The blood bank's medical officer does the final screening. Once the hospital records your donation, it appears on your impact page.

What each side can see

  • Before a donor pledges, hospitals see only blood group, approximate position and distance.
  • After pledging, the hospital sees the donor's name, contact details and live ETA.
  • Donors never see patient names or clinical notes.
