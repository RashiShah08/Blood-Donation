Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

What we collect

  • Donors: name, email, optional phone number, date of birth, gender, weight, blood group, any declared health condition, home location, availability and last donation date.
  • Hospitals: name, contact details, address, location and hospital type.
  • Requests: patient name, gender, optional weight, blood group, units, urgency and notes, entered by the hospital.
  • Activity: pledges, donation outcomes, which alerts were sent, and the latest arrival time and distance while a donor uses directions.

We don't store your answers to the eligibility questions, or your live position.

How it's used

  • To match donors with compatible requests nearby, and to work out when a donor can donate again.
  • To send donation alerts and password reset emails.
  • To show hospitals who has pledged for their requests and roughly when they'll arrive.

Who can see what

  • Before you pledge, a hospital sees only your blood group, your approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) and your distance. It doesn't see your name or contact details.
  • When you pledge for a request, that hospital sees your name, email and phone number so it can coordinate your donation.
  • While you use directions to the hospital, it sees your estimated arrival time and the distance left, never your position.
  • Donors never see patient names or clinical notes.
  • We don't sell or share personal data for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • OpenStreetMap map tiles are loaded by your browser, so OpenStreetMap receives your IP address.
  • OpenRouteService receives your current position and the hospital's position (not your identity) when you request directions.
  • An email provider delivers alerts and password reset emails.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies, and forms are protected against cross-site request forgery. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Cookies and notifications

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Desktop notifications are only shown if you turn them on in your browser.

Your choices

  • Update your details or location on your profile page at any time.
  • Turn off availability to stop receiving alerts.
  • Delete your donor account from your profile page. This permanently removes your personal data and pledges.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub.
