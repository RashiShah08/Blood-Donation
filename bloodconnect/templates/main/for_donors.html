Donor guidelines

Everything you need before you give blood

Based on India's National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) donor selection guidelines. The blood bank's medical officer always makes the final decision.

{{ ui.icon('check') }} Who can donate

  • Aged {{ facts.min_age }} to {{ facts.max_age }} years
  • Weigh at least {{ facts.min_weight }} kg
  • Haemoglobin of at least 12.5 g/dL (checked at the blood bank)
  • Feeling healthy, with no fever or infection
  • At least {{ facts.male_interval }} days (men) or {{ facts.female_interval }} days (women) since your last whole blood donation

{{ ui.icon('clock') }} When to wait

  • 15 days after a cold, fever, antibiotics or most vaccines
  • 3 months after malaria
  • 6 months after dengue, chikungunya, minor surgery or a tooth extraction
  • 12 months after major surgery, a tattoo, a piercing or a transfusion
  • During pregnancy, breastfeeding or within a year of giving birth

Before you donate

  • Eat a normal meal and drink plenty of water in the hours before.
  • Get a good night's sleep and avoid alcohol for 24 hours.
  • Carry a photo ID and a list of any medicines you take.
  • Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily.

At the blood bank

Expect registration, a short health check (weight, blood pressure, pulse and haemoglobin) and a private interview. The donation itself usually takes 8 to 10 minutes. Plan for about an hour in total.

After you donate

  • Rest for 10 to 15 minutes and have the refreshments offered.
  • Drink extra fluids and avoid heavy exercise for the rest of the day.
  • If you feel faint, lie down with your feet raised.
  • If you feel unwell in the following days, call the blood bank.

Donation is voluntary and unpaid. It's against the law in India to sell blood or pay donors, and BloodConnect never does.
